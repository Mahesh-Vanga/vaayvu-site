Deep dive into each feature

Four integrated capabilities that replace a dozen extensions.

Rasha Shield — Local-first ad & tracker blocking

Written for Vaayvu from the ground up. No EasyList, uBlock Origin, or AdGuard rule sets. Rules compile to bytecode and match on-device — page content never leaves your machine.

Vaayvu Sync — Your bookmarks. Your server. No middleman.

A lightweight sync service you run yourself. Bookmarks and regular open tabs encrypt locally (AES-GCM, Argon2id keys), transmit to your sync server, decrypt on the other end. No Locathara accounts, no telemetry, no cloud.

India Search — Optional on-device mode

Replaces ordinary web search with curated, on-device directories for government services, pincodes, IFSC codes, STD codes, and more. No network requests until you choose a result.
