Privacy Policy

Vaayvu Browser collects no personal data. No telemetry. No accounts required. Self-hosted sync encrypts locally. This policy explains what we don't collect.

Summary

Vaayvu Browser does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting to our servers, no usage analytics, no unique identifiers, and no accounts required to use the browser.

If you choose to use Vaayvu Sync, your data is end-to-end encrypted on your device before it ever leaves your machine. The sync server (which you host yourself) stores only ciphertext.

This policy covers the Vaayvu Browser application and the Locathara website.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No browsing history, URLs, or page content
  • No search queries or autocomplete suggestions
  • No IP addresses, device fingerprints, or unique identifiers
  • No crash reports or error logs sent to our servers
  • No usage statistics, feature engagement, or retention metrics
  • No account data (because there are no accounts)
  • No email addresses, names, or contact information (unless you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter)

Local Data Storage

The following data stays on your device and is never transmitted to Locathara:

  • Browser data: History, bookmarks, cookies, cache, permissions, form autofill — stored in platform-native storage (IndexedDB, Keychain, Credential Manager, Keystore)
  • Rasha Shield: Compiled rule bytecode, per-site shield state, block counters — stored locally
  • Spaces: Tab groups, pinned tabs, space themes — stored locally
  • Settings: Preferences, themes, keyboard shortcuts — stored locally
  • Vaayvu Sync keys: Your encryption keys (derived from passphrase via Argon2id) never leave your device

You can clear all local data at any time via the browser's clear data dialog or by uninstalling the application.

Vaayvu Sync (Optional)

Vaayvu Sync is an optional, self-hosted synchronization service. If you choose to use it:

  • Encryption: All data encrypts on your device using AES-GCM with keys derived from your passphrase via Argon2id (t=3, m=64MB, p=4) before transmission
  • Server storage: Your self-hosted sync server stores only ciphertext + nonces. It cannot read your bookmarks, tabs, or history
  • No Locathara access: We do not operate sync servers for users. You host your own. We have zero access to your sync data
  • Data synced: Bookmarks and regular (non-private) open tabs only. Private windows are excluded by design
  • Open protocol: The sync protocol is documented and open. You can build your own client or server implementation

Third-Party Content

When you visit websites, those sites may set their own cookies, run their own scripts, and make their own network requests. Vaayvu's Rasha Shield blocks many third-party trackers and ads by default, but cannot prevent all first-party data collection by the sites you choose to visit.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of websites you visit.

Children's Privacy

Vaayvu Browser is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect no personal information at all, this is inherently satisfied.

Security

  • All sync traffic uses TLS 1.2+
  • Local data encrypted at rest via platform keychain/keystore
  • Sync encryption keys derived via Argon2id (memory-hard KDF)
  • Per-record AES-GCM with unique nonces
  • No hardcoded keys, no backdoors, no key escrow
  • Open source — audit the code yourself at github.com/Mahesh-Vanga/vaayvu-site

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the browser evolves. Material changes will be noted in release notes and on this page. The "last updated" date at the bottom indicates the current version.

Continued use of Vaayvu after policy changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
